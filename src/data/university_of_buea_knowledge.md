## 4. Course Registration & Credit System Regulations (Statute 4)

### Credit Values & Workload
- **Definition:** 1 Credit Unit represents approximately 10 to 12 hours of lectures/tutorials plus practicals and individual study.
- **Standard Semester Load:** 30 to 36 Credit Units per semester (60 to 72 credits per academic year).
- **Minimum Semester Load:** 30 Credit Units.
- **Maximum Standard Load:** 36 Credit Units.

### Additional Credits Policy Criteria
- **Question:** Can an enrolled student register for more than 36 credits in a single semester?
- **Official Policy:** **YES**, an enrolled student with a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of **3.0 or higher** may apply to register up to **6 additional credit units** (maximum total of 42 credits in that semester).
- **Approval Flow:** Requires written recommendation from the Head of Department (HOD) and executive approval by the Faculty Dean.
- **Purpose:** Granted to clear trailing carryover courses or accelerate graduation requirements.

### Registration Procedure (Form A & B)
1. **Fee Settlement:** Pay at least 60% first installment fee at an authorized bank.
2. **Online Selection:** Log in to student portal -> Select compulsory core courses and electives up to permitted credit cap.
3. **Adviser Validation:** Print 3 copies of Form A/B and submit to Departmental Academic Adviser for signature.
4. **Filing:** Deposit 1 copy with HOD, 1 copy at Faculty Registry, and keep 1 stamped copy.

## 5. Examinations & Grading Regulations

### Continuous Assessment & Final Exam Weighting
- **Continuous Assessment (CA):** 30% of total course mark (Tests, laboratory reports, attendance, assignments).
- **Final Written Examination:** 70% of total course mark.
- **75% Attendance Requirement:** Students must attend at least 75% of scheduled lectures and practicals to sit for the final exam.

### Grading Scale & Grade Point Equivalents
- **Grade A (80 - 100%):** GPA 4.0 — Excellent
- **Grade B+ (70 - 79%):** GPA 3.5 — Very Good
- **Grade B (60 - 69%):** GPA 3.0 — Good
- **Grade C+ (55 - 59%):** GPA 2.5 — Fairly Good
- **Grade C (50 - 54%):** GPA 2.0 — Pass
- **Grade D (40 - 49%):** GPA 1.0 — Eligible for Supplementary Resit (Rattrapage)
- **Grade F (Below 40%):** GPA 0.0 — Fail (Must retake complete course)
